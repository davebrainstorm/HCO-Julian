HCO — Logo design · Round 2Process · 02/16
Process
Found in the terrain.
Drawn by hand.
1 · One dataset

Band-limited fractal terrain with ridged, anisotropic ranges. Illustrative and procedural: it is not real elevation data and implies no location.

2 · 2,379 samples

Sampled from above (heightmaps, contours) and from the side (profiles), then filtered against the brief: ≤ 8 × 8 cells, one form, asymmetric, a small summit.

3 · 25 drawn, 3 survive

Redrawn by hand on the grid in clean runs. Contour rings read as eyes; most heightmaps read as blobs. Three studies go forward.

What the test changed

The brief expected the view from above to win. It didn't. A side view survives the ziggurat and bar-chart traps once it plots the surface — one measured line — instead of filling the mass. That is study S3, Ridgeline.

HCO — Logo design · Round 2Study 3 · Ridgeline · 05/16
Study 3 · Profile, plotted as a measured line
Ridgeline
Recommended
8 × 5 grid · 8 cells · one module
16 px ×6
Actual 16 · 24 · 32 px, pixel-aligned

The ridge in section, plotted the way an elevation profile is: one measured cell per step, coloured by height. A long approach, the summit in Chalk, a short steep drop. Drawn with the same weight as the letters — one cell is one stem.

Strengths
  • Reads at once as a mountain and as measured data — and not as a pyramid or a bar chart.
  • Pixel-perfect at 16 px; the line and the letters share one stroke.
  • Not found among existing pixel-mountain marks (search recorded, not clearance).
Weaknesses
  • In one colour it is close to a rising-line chart; the drop after the summit and the context have to do the work.
  • Light next to the bold HCO at very small sizes — use the compact lockup below 120 px.

HCO — Logo design · Round 2Background · 11/16
Background
One dataset, three renderings
A · Pixel heightmap

Cells coloured by the ramp, lit by the relief. The symbol at full resolution — the hero mode.

B · Dot matrix

Dot size is elevation. A quieter, more editorial texture for reports and covers.

C · Quantised hillshade

Relief in six field tones, no signal colour. The quietest: safe under long text.

Rules

Logo sits in the quiet lowland, never over a ridge. Text over terrain measures ≥ 4.5:1 against the busiest area beneath it. No glow, blur or cinematic lighting.

Data

Procedural and labelled “Illustrative terrain study” until a real region is confirmed; then Copernicus GLO-30 (attributed) or USGS 3DEP. No place names or coordinates.
