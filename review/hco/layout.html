Layout and space

One module everywhere: an 8 px sample for space and size, a 12-column grid, square corners and 45° angles.

8 px sample12 / 8 / 4 columnsRadius 0

One module #

In print the module is 1M, the width of one symbol sample. On screen the sample is 8 px: space, sizes and the baseline are all counted in samples or half-samples.

Grid #

A 12-column grid on desktop, 8 on tablet and 4 on phones. Content aligns to columns; space between components comes from the spacing scale.

Desktop · 12 columns · margin 80 · gutter 24
Tablet · 8 columns · margin 40 · gutter 16
Phone · 4 columns · margin 16 · gutter 16
